# Learning curriculum: Cloud Business Office Lead

Build a cloud-adoption decision pack linking business value, ownership, costs and controls.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Leadership, product and technology relationships](professions-leadership.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Cloud Business Office Lead](professions-leadership.md#cloud-business-office-lead)

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Has participated in cloud adoption or service governance with business and technology stakeholders.
- Can interpret usage and spending data and distinguish estimates from commitments.

**What you will produce:** A cloud operating-model proposal and reviewed adoption decision using explicit financial and service assumptions.

**Resource coverage:** 2 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Understand why cloud is being considered](#stage-1)
2. [Build a transparent economic case](#stage-2)

4. [Review an adoption portfolio decision](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand why cloud is being considered

**Learn:**

- Business drivers
- Stakeholder needs
- Adoption boundaries

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Open Practice Library](https://github.com/openpracticelibrary/openpracticelibrary) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-design-product.md#resource-open-practice-library).

**Practise:** Facilitate a small discovery exercise for a fictional or consented service. Record the desired outcome, constraints and which needs are not automatically solved by moving to cloud.

**Advance when:** The brief names a business reason, affected stakeholders and assumptions requiring evidence rather than treating migration itself as the goal.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Build a transparent economic case

**Learn:**

- Cost drivers
- Demand uncertainty
- Total ownership costs

**Resource still needed:** A verified cloud financial-management or FinOps curriculum and current provider cost documentation are not yet included.

**Practise:** Compare keeping one service in its current environment with a cloud option. Model a few usage scenarios, including support, migration and exit costs, with all numbers labelled as estimates.

**Advance when:** The model exposes its inputs and shows how a demand change can reverse the preferred option.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Define an operating and governance model

**Learn:**

- Decision rights
- Service ownership
- Controls and exceptions

**Resource still needed:** Verified cloud governance and organisational operating-model guidance is still needed; Kubernetes exam objectives would not cover this stage.

**Practise:** Draft the relationships between product owners, platform teams, finance and risk specialists. Describe how a team requests a service, reports costs and escalates an exception.

**Advance when:** A walkthrough identifies who can approve an adoption decision and who owns its running costs, reliability and control evidence.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Review an adoption portfolio decision

**Learn:**

- Prioritisation
- Benefits review
- Exit strategy

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Markdown Architectural Decision Records (MADR)](https://github.com/adr/madr) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-architecture.md#resource-madr).

**Practise:** Compare two candidate workloads, record which should proceed first and define a review point. Attach the economic model, governance map and a realistic rollback or exit assumption.

**Advance when:** The packet provides a defensible adoption order, accountable owners and explicit conditions to continue, change or stop.

## After this recipe

Develop real cloud operating-model and financial-review experience with qualified specialists before leading an adoption office.
