<a id="resource-cs50x"></a>
<details><summary>CS50x - Harvard&#x27;s Introduction to Computer Science: requirements and source notes</summary>

**Topic level:** beginner. Read the requirements below before choosing it.

**Languages recorded:** en, es, pt

**Useful for:**

- Free CS50 Certificate available (70% on each problem set and final project)
- Weekly lectures with problem sets and a final project
- Large community (Discord, Ed, Reddit)
- Spanish and Portuguese pages linked from the site (CS50x en espanol)

**Requirements, costs and limitations:**

- Problem sets are demanding for complete beginners
- Spanish and Portuguese lectures are dubbed by an AI service (lingopal.ai, per the site banner); other materials not checked in those languages
- Verified edX certificate costs money

**Source checked:** 2026-10-21 (official-site).

cs50.harvard.edu/x loads (CS50x 2026); certificate page confirms free CS50 Certificate; FAQs updated 2026-10-14

**Study format:** guided. **Focus-friendly rating:** unknown (editorial judgment).

</details>
