# Learning curriculum: Statistician

An editorial route through probability, study design, inference and a transparent statistical investigation.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Data, analytics and artificial intelligence](professions-data-ai.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Statistician](professions-data-ai.md#statistician)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Algebra and introductory calculus
- Basic programming or willingness to learn Python
- Comfort interpreting tables

**What you will produce:** A statistical report with a defined estimand, documented assumptions, uncertainty and sensitivity analysis.

**Resource coverage:** 4 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Connect probability to sampling variation](#stage-1)
2. [Design the comparison before inspecting outcomes](#stage-2)
3. [Estimate effects and check assumptions](#stage-3)
4. [Capstone - deliver a reproducible statistical report](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Connect probability to sampling variation

**Learn:**

- Random variables and distributions
- Sampling and simulation
- Expectation and variation

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-seeing-theory).
- [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-openintro-statistics).

**Practise:** Simulate repeated samples and compare their summaries with the population you specified.

**Advance when:** Explain why individual variation differs from uncertainty in an estimated mean.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Design the comparison before inspecting outcomes

**Learn:**

- Population and estimand
- Randomization and confounding
- Measurement and missingness

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-openintro-statistics).

**Practise:** Draft a study plan that states the comparison, measurements, exclusions and intended analysis.

**Advance when:** Identify a confounder and explain which conclusions the design can and cannot support.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Estimate effects and check assumptions

**Learn:**

- Intervals and hypothesis tests
- Regression diagnostics
- Bayesian updating

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Think Stats (3rd edition)](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkStats/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-think-stats).
- [Think Bayes (2nd edition)](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkBayes2/) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-think-bayes).

**Practise:** Analyze a permitted small dataset with one primary method and one clearly justified sensitivity check.

**Advance when:** Interpret an interval or posterior correctly and report a diagnostic that challenges the model assumptions.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone - deliver a reproducible statistical report

**Learn:**

- Analysis specification
- Uncertainty communication
- Limitations and reproducible computation

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Project Jupyter](https://jupyter.org/) — all in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-project-jupyter).
- [storytelling with data: free resources](https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/resources) — beginner in this topic; freemium access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-storytelling-with-data-resources).

**Practise:** Produce a report separating the study design, analysis decisions, result and threats to validity.

**Advance when:** Regenerate the tables from code and explain why statistical significance alone is insufficient.

## After this recipe

Add advanced probability, experimental design and domain-specific methodology; formal statistical practice requires more than this project.
