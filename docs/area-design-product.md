## Selected repositories

- [GOV.UK Design System](https://github.com/alphagov/govuk-design-system): A public design-system reference with component examples and usage guidance for learning how to make consistent, understandable service interfaces. (intermediate; Free)
- [Open Practice Library](https://github.com/openpracticelibrary/openpracticelibrary): A collaborative collection of product discovery and team practices with explanations and facilitation guidance, including empathy mapping to explore user needs. (intermediate; Free)
