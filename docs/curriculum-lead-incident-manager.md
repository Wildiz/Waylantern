# Learning curriculum: Lead Incident Manager

Improve incident coordination across teams using evidence from multiple cases rather than one outage.

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Have repeated incident-coordination experience.
- Understand service ownership, review practices and escalation policies.

**What you will produce:** A cross-incident improvement proposal with measurable findings, a coaching exercise and governance follow-up.

**Resource coverage:** 0 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Compare incident records fairly

**Learn:**

- Consistent event definitions
- Data completeness and bias

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed incident-programme analytics or leadership curriculum is currently catalogued.

**Practise:** Build a fictional set of incident timelines and define detection, ownership, mitigation and restoration timestamps consistently.

**Advance when:** The dataset identifies missing evidence and does not treat incomparable durations as a reliable team ranking.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Find coordination weaknesses

**Learn:**

- Handoff quality
- Escalation bottlenecks

**Resource still needed:** A reviewed method for cross-incident organisational diagnosis is missing; the analysis is an editorial exercise.

**Practise:** Analyse the records for delayed ownership or contradictory updates and propose a testable process improvement.

**Advance when:** The improvement targets an observed coordination failure rather than assigning blame to an individual.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Coach coordinators and rehearse policy

**Learn:**

- Scenario facilitation
- Decision authority and feedback

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed incident-leadership training or employer-approved simulation policy is catalogued.

**Practise:** Design a tabletop that tests the proposed handoff improvement and give concrete feedback on the participants' decisions.

**Advance when:** The exercise has observable success criteria and distinguishes coaching feedback from policy approval.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone: evaluate an incident-programme change

**Learn:**

- Before/after measures
- Governance and action tracking

**Resource still needed:** This is an editorial leadership proposal, not proof of improved production incident performance or compliance with a framework.

**Practise:** Present the baseline evidence, exercise findings and a proposed rollout with an owner, review date and rollback condition.

**Advance when:** A reviewer can see how the change would be evaluated and what evidence could disprove its value.

## After this recipe

Validate the proposal with experienced service owners and actual governance before changing incident policy.
