# Learning curriculum: IT Auditor

An assurance recipe for testing technology controls against explicitly selected criteria and documenting defensible findings.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Cybersecurity, audit and risk](professions-security.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [IT Auditor](professions-security.md#it-auditor)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Basic IT operations and account-management concepts.
- Ability to read policies and keep evidence organized.

**What you will produce:** A mock IT audit workpaper packet with criteria, sampling rationale, evidence and reviewed findings.

**Resource coverage:** 1 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand systems and audit scope

**Learn:**

- IT processes and control ownership
- Audit objectives, independence and criteria

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Professor Messer (CompTIA Network+ and Security+ videos)](https://www.professormesser.com/security-plus/sy0-701/sy0-701-video/sy0-701-comptia-security-plus-course/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-devops.md#resource-professor-messer).

**Practise:** Map a fictional user-access process and agree a narrow audit objective with a peer acting as the process owner.

**Advance when:** The scope identifies the process, period, criteria and exclusions without claiming compliance with an unreviewed standard.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Design control tests

**Learn:**

- Control design versus operation
- Population, sample selection and evidence requests

**Resource still needed:** No verified IT audit methodology or professional auditing standard has been catalogued; criteria and sampling here are practice examples.

**Practise:** Write an editorial access-approval criterion and test plan, then create synthetic account-request and approval records.

**Advance when:** The plan explains which evidence would support the conclusion and how the sample was chosen.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Evaluate evidence and findings

**Learn:**

- Traceability and contradictory evidence
- Condition, criterion, cause and consequence

**Resource still needed:** No formal assurance opinion or standard-specific interpretation is supported by the current catalogue.

**Practise:** Compare the synthetic records with your criterion; document one passing case, one exception and one case with insufficient evidence.

**Advance when:** Each conclusion cites the exact record and distinguishes a deviation from an inability to test.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Deliver an auditable workpaper packet

**Learn:**

- Finding review and management response
- Remediation follow-up and file organization

**Resource still needed:** This editorial audit simulation is not a professional audit qualification or a real assurance engagement.

**Practise:** Assemble the scope, test plan, evidence index and findings, then have a peer challenge the conclusions and revise them.

**Advance when:** A reviewer can trace findings to tests, see limitations and identify owners for follow-up without reopening undocumented assumptions.

## After this recipe

Study the audit methodology and criteria actually used by an employer with a qualified supervisor before performing real assurance work.
