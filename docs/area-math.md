## Courses and books

| Name | Cost | Level | Guided? | Focus-friendly | Languages | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Grant Sanderson's video series that explains derivatives, integrals and the fundamental theorem of calculus visually, with text adaptations of the lessons on the website. |
| [3Blue1Brown: Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Grant Sanderson's video series that builds geometric intuition for vectors, linear transformations, matrices, determinants and eigenvectors. |
| [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/) | Free + paid extras | beginner | guided | - | en | Interactive, problem-based lessons in math and coding with a built-in AI tutor, organized into topics, units and concepts. |
| [Discrete Mathematics: An Open Introduction (Oscar Levin)](https://discrete.openmathbooks.org/dmoi4.html) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Free open-source textbook for a first or second year discrete math course, covering logic, proofs, graph theory, counting and sequences, with interactive exercises on Runestone Academy. |
| [Khan Academy Math (linear algebra, statistics, calculus)](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Khan Academy's math library, including linear algebra, statistics and probability, and calculus courses with videos, articles and practice exercises. |
| [Mathematics for Computer Science (MIT 6.042 text)](https://courses.csail.mit.edu/6.042/spring18/mcs.pdf) | Free | intermediate | guided | - | en | Free textbook by Lehman, Leighton and Meyer used in MIT 6.042 covering proofs, discrete structures, counting and discrete probability. |
| [Mathematics for Machine Learning (book)](https://mml-book.github.io/) | Free | intermediate | guided | - | en | Companion site of the Cambridge University Press book covering linear algebra, analytic geometry, matrix decompositions, vector calculus, probability and optimization, plus linear regression, PCA, GMMs and SVMs. |
| [MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06 Linear Algebra (Gilbert Strang)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06-linear-algebra-spring-2010/) | Free | intermediate | guided | - | en | MIT's undergraduate linear algebra course taught by Gilbert Strang, with lecture videos, readings, problem sets, exams and solutions. |
| [OpenIntro Statistics](https://www.openintro.org/book/os/) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Open-license introductory statistics textbook from OpenIntro with free PDF, video lectures, slides, labs and data sets. |
| [Seeing Theory](https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/) | Free | beginner | self-directed | - | en, zh, es | Brown University's interactive visual introduction to probability and statistics, from basic probability and distributions to Bayesian inference and regression. |
| [StatQuest with Josh Starmer](https://statquest.org) | Free | beginner | self-directed | high | en | Video explanations of statistics, machine learning, and neural networks, from linear regression to attention, with the author's book and store as optional paid extras. |
| [Think Bayes (2nd edition)](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkBayes2/) | Free | intermediate | guided | medium | en | Free online introduction to Bayesian statistics using computational methods in Python, from Bayes's theorem through MCMC and approximate Bayesian computation, with Colab notebooks. |
| [Think Stats (3rd edition)](https://allendowney.github.io/ThinkStats/) | Free | beginner | guided | medium | en | Free online book introducing probability and statistics to Python programmers through exploratory data analysis, distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing and regression, with runnable notebooks. |
