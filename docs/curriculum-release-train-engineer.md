# Learning curriculum: Release Train Engineer

Coordinate a multi-team delivery plan with visible dependencies, capacity limits and inspection points.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Operations, platforms and IT support](professions-operations.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Release Train Engineer](professions-operations.md#release-train-engineer)

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Have experience facilitating delivery across multiple teams.
- Understand iteration planning, work tracking and stakeholder constraints.

**What you will produce:** A fictional integrated delivery plan with dependencies, risks, a facilitation record and revised commitments.

**Resource coverage:** 0 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Map value and dependencies

**Learn:**

- Shared outcomes
- Team interfaces and sequencing

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed release-train or named scaling-framework curriculum is catalogued; terms are used descriptively.

**Practise:** Describe a small fictional product increment spanning three teams and map the dependencies blocking delivery.

**Advance when:** Every dependency has a provider, consumer and required decision or deliverable.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Facilitate a realistic plan

**Learn:**

- Capacity and uncertainty
- Risk and ownership

**Resource still needed:** A reviewed multi-team programme planning or release-train facilitation manual is missing.

**Practise:** Run a tabletop planning session using invented team capacities and external constraints.

**Advance when:** The plan avoids double-counted capacity and records unresolved risks and who will address them.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Inspect integration and adapt

**Learn:**

- Integrated evidence
- Dependency changes

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed source supplies an official integration cadence or event structure for this role.

**Practise:** Introduce a delayed dependency and revise the plan using a concrete integration check rather than status claims alone.

**Advance when:** Teams can identify the changed commitment, delivery impact and next integration evidence.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone: rehearse an integrated delivery review

**Learn:**

- Facilitation and decisions
- Improvement actions

**Resource still needed:** This editorial delivery-coordination simulation is not an official curriculum from any branded scaling framework.

**Practise:** Present the original plan, simulated integration results, changed dependencies and an improvement proposal.

**Advance when:** The review traces outcomes to evidence and makes ownership clear without presenting the exercise as framework certification.

## After this recipe

Choose the actual delivery model used by the organisation and study its authorised guidance with experienced facilitators.
