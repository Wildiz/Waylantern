## Tools and apps

| Name | Cost | Level | Guided? | Focus-friendly | Languages | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Cold Turkey Blocker](https://getcoldturkey.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en | Website and application blocker for Windows and macOS with a free version for timed website blocks and a one-time-purchase Pro version. |
| [FLOWN](https://www.flown.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en | Virtual coworking platform with scheduled live focus sessions for body doubling, aimed at remote workers, students and people with ADHD. |
| [Focus Timer (GNOME Pomodoro)](https://github.com/focustimerhq/FocusTimer) | Free | all | self-directed | high | en | Open-source time-management utility for GNOME based on the Pomodoro technique. |
| [Focusmate](https://www.focusmate.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en | Virtual coworking service that pairs you on video with another member for a silent, timed work session in which you each state your goal. |
| [Forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | medium | en | Pomodoro-style focus timer in which a virtual tree grows while you stay off your phone and dies if you leave, with apps and a Chrome/Firefox extension. |
| [Freedom](https://freedom.to/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en | Cross-device app and website blocker (Mac, Windows, iOS, Android, Chromebook) with a free tier and a paid Premium plan adding scheduling and locked mode. |
| [Goblin Tools](https://goblin.tools/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en | Collection of small AI-assisted web tools for neurodivergent users, such as Magic ToDo, which breaks a task into smaller steps, plus an estimator and a braindump compiler. |
| [Habitica](https://habitica.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | medium | en, es, de, fr, pt | Open-source habit and to-do app that turns tasks into a role-playing game with points, rewards and optional parties. |
| [LeechBlock NG](https://www.proginosko.com/leechblock/) | Free | all | self-directed | high | en | Free browser extension for Firefox, Chrome, Edge and Chromium browsers that blocks chosen sites by schedule, time limit or lockdown. |
| [Pomofocus](https://pomofocus.io/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | medium | en | Browser-based customizable Pomodoro timer with a task list, usable on desktop and mobile, with some extras behind a premium tier. |
| [Pomotroid](https://github.com/Splode/pomotroid) | Free | all | self-directed | medium | en | Open-source desktop Pomodoro timer with configurable intervals, statistics, themes and a WebSocket API, built with Tauri, Rust and Svelte. |
| [StudyStream (formerly Study Together)](https://studystream.live/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | medium | en | Online community of students with always-on virtual study rooms, timers and goals; Study Together merged into StudyStream and new users are asked to sign up there. |
| [Super Productivity](https://super-productivity.com/) | Free | all | self-directed | high | en | Free open-source to-do and time-tracking app with timeboxing, a Pomodoro timer and calendar/Jira/GitHub task import, with a web app and desktop downloads. |
| [The Pomodoro Technique (official site)](https://www.pomodorotechnique.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | medium | en | Official site of Francesco Cirillo's Pomodoro Technique with free tools and worksheets and optional paid online learning and cohorts. |
| [Tiimo](https://www.tiimoapp.com/) | Free + paid extras | all | self-directed | high | en, de, fr, es, pt, ko, ja | Visual daily planner for neurodivergent users with timers, to-do lists, AI-generated step-by-step plans and a focus timer on iOS, Android and web. |
| [TomatoBar](https://github.com/ivoronin/TomatoBar) | Free | all | self-directed | high | en | Open-source Pomodoro timer that lives in the macOS menu bar. |
