# Learning curriculum: Major Incident Manager

Coordinate a high-impact multi-team outage using decision discipline and explicit recovery evidence.

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

## Before you start

- Have experience coordinating ordinary incidents.
- Understand business impact, technical ownership and executive communication.

**What you will produce:** A multi-team outage exercise with a command structure, decision log, stakeholder updates and recovery sign-off.

**Resource coverage:** 0 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

## 1. Declare and scope a major incident

**Learn:**

- Business impact
- Activation criteria

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed major-incident framework or current organisation-specific thresholds are catalogued.

**Practise:** Define fictional activation thresholds and use them to classify two outage reports with different business impacts.

**Advance when:** The declaration cites impact and decision authority rather than just the number of technical alerts.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Set the coordination structure

**Learn:**

- Incident command roles
- Parallel workstreams

**Resource still needed:** A reviewed guide for major-incident command and cross-team escalation is missing.

**Practise:** Assign a coordinator, technical leads, recorder and communications owner for a fictional two-service outage.

**Advance when:** Workstreams have explicit owners and coordination points, with one shared decision record.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Manage uncertainty and recovery choices

**Learn:**

- Option and risk records
- Executive and user updates

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed major-outage decision or stakeholder-communications source supports a formal prescribed method here.

**Practise:** Compare a workaround with a riskier repair and write separate concise updates for users and decision-makers.

**Advance when:** The recommendation identifies evidence, reversibility and unresolved risk without inventing a restoration deadline.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone: rehearse a multi-service outage

**Learn:**

- Recovery sign-off
- Learning review

**Resource still needed:** The whole exercise is editorial; fictional verification does not establish live-service recovery or a professional qualification.

**Practise:** Facilitate a timed tabletop with changing injects and end with service-specific verification and a shared review.

**Advance when:** The timeline shows who made each recovery decision and each follow-up action has an accountable owner.

## After this recipe

Shadow major-incident coordination and practise the organisation's actual notification, authority and continuity requirements.
