## Full paths (a free university)

| Name | Cost | Level | Guided? | Focus-friendly | Languages | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/) | Free | all | self-directed | - | en | MIT's free collection of lecture notes, exams, problem sets and videos from over 2,500 MIT courses across the whole curriculum, including EECS. |
| [Stanford Engineering Everywhere](https://see.stanford.edu/) | Free | intermediate | guided | - | en | Free full course videos, handouts, homework and exams from Stanford, including the three-course intro CS sequence (CS106A, CS106B, CS107), Machine Learning (CS229) and linear systems and optimization courses. |

## Courses and books

| Name | Cost | Level | Guided? | Focus-friendly | Languages | What it is |

| [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/) | Free + paid extras | all | guided | - | en | Course platform hosting university and company courses (e.g. Princeton Algorithms, Nand2Tetris) where free access, trials or financial aid vary by course and certificates are paid. |
| [Khan Academy Computing](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Khan Academy's computing section with beginner programming and computer science courses, including an Intro to Computer Science course in Python released for the 2024-25 school year. |
| [Khan Academy Math (linear algebra, statistics, calculus)](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) | Free | beginner | guided | - | en | Khan Academy's math library, including linear algebra, statistics and probability, and calculus courses with videos, articles and practice exercises. |
| [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/) | Free | all | semi | - | en | Microsoft's free training catalogue of learning paths and modules on Azure, Microsoft 365, Power Platform, security and developer tools, with role-based paths and credentials. |
| [OpenLearn (The Open University)](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/) | Free | beginner | semi | - | en | The Open University's free learning platform with short courses including computing and IT topics, offering a free statement of participation after completion. |
