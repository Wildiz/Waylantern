# Learning curriculum: Computer and Information Systems Manager

Balance information-service demand, operational evidence and team capacity in a bounded service portfolio.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Leadership, product and technology relationships](professions-leadership.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Computer and Information Systems Manager](professions-leadership.md#computer-information-systems-manager)

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Has delivered or supported organisational information systems.
- Has coordinated people or service work and can interpret basic service measures.

**What you will produce:** A service portfolio and management review demonstrating accountable service and resource decisions.

**Resource coverage:** 2 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Understand service users and ownership](#stage-1)
2. [Connect service evidence with resources](#stage-2)
3. [Manage a service change and continuity concern](#stage-3)
4. [Run a management review](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand service users and ownership

**Learn:**

- Service catalogue
- Demand
- Ownership

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-glpi).

**Practise:** Build a synthetic catalogue of three information services with users, owners, assets and support responsibilities. Record how requests and incidents are routed.

**Advance when:** A reader can identify the owner and support route of each service and distinguish a new request from an operational failure.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Connect service evidence with resources

**Learn:**

- Capacity
- Service measures
- Skills coverage

**Resource still needed:** A verified information-systems management and workforce-planning curriculum is missing from the catalogue.

**Practise:** Create a weekly sample of service demand and incidents, then map it to team capacity and skills. Identify a bottleneck without reducing people to ticket counts.

**Advance when:** Your review separates demand, available capacity and outcomes, and identifies the assumptions behind one resource decision.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Manage a service change and continuity concern

**Learn:**

- Change impact
- Continuity
- Supplier dependencies

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Markdown Architectural Decision Records (MADR)](https://github.com/adr/madr) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-architecture.md#resource-madr).

**Practise:** Choose one service change and record options, dependencies, continuity implications and decision ownership. Include a supplier assumption requiring validation.

**Advance when:** The decision names the change benefit, potential disruption, contingency and the party responsible for each follow-up.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Run a management review

**Learn:**

- Accountability
- Prioritisation
- Improvement tracking

**Resource still needed:** Verified material on service governance, people management and organisational reporting is still required.

**Practise:** Assemble the catalogue, service evidence, capacity view and change decision into a management review. Prioritise one improvement and define how its outcome will be checked.

**Advance when:** The capstone produces an accountable improvement decision and a review plan supported by evidence rather than a summary of completed activities.

## After this recipe

Practise management reviews with an experienced service owner and deepen the sector-specific governance and people-management material.
