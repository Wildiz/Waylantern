## Pros and cons

<details><summary>Show pros and cons for each entry</summary>

**Grind 75**

- Good: Schedule builder based on your available time
- Good: Successor to the widely shared Blind 75 list
- Good: Part of the free Tech Interview Handbook
- Watch out: Links out to problems on third-party sites such as LeetCode
- Watch out: Interview-prep focus

**LLM Course (Maxime Labonne)**

- Good: Clear three-part roadmap with linked notebooks
- Good: Author states the course will always stay free
- Watch out: Links to resources rather than being a single course
- Watch out: Last repo commit February 2026

**MDN Curriculum**

- Good: Vendor-neutral roadmap for front-end work
- Good: Linked to MDN reference and Learn modules
- Watch out: Front-end only
- Watch out: Mostly points to other resources rather than teaching itself

**roadmap.sh**

- Good: Good for seeing the whole map when you do not know where to start
- Good: Covers AI and data roadmaps
- Watch out: Shows what to learn
- Watch out: not how to learn it

**Teach Yourself Computer Science**

- Good: Opinionated and short - tells you what to study and in what order
- Good: Pairs a book and a video series per subject
- Good: Many community translations
- Watch out: Is a reading list, not a platform - no exercises or feedback of its own
- Watch out: Some recommended books must be bought
- Watch out: Last major update was May 2020 per the page

**Tech Interview Handbook**

- Good: Ready-made study plans
- Good: MIT licensed
- Watch out: Career-prep focus rather than general learning
- Watch out: README promotes paid third-party courses via affiliate links

</details>
