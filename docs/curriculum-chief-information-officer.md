# Learning curriculum: Chief Information Officer

Develop an information-systems strategy and an evidence-based investment and governance proposal.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Leadership, product and technology relationships](professions-leadership.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Chief Information Officer](professions-leadership.md#chief-information-officer)

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Has substantial experience delivering or operating organisational information systems.
- Has managed budgets, stakeholders and service or delivery teams.
- Understands the organisation sector and can obtain qualified financial, legal and security advice.

**What you will produce:** A strategic information-services portfolio case with investment choices, governance and outcome measures.

**Resource coverage:** 1 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

1. [Understand organisational needs and the current information estate](#stage-1)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand organisational needs and the current information estate

**Learn:**

- Organisational objectives
- Information flows
- Service portfolio

**Resource still needed:** No verified CIO or information-systems leadership curriculum is catalogued; sector-specific governance and information-management sources are required.

**Practise:** For an organisation you know or a labelled case, map key outcomes to information-dependent services, owners and major constraints. Separate known facts from assumptions.

**Advance when:** The current-state brief links services and information to business outcomes and identifies ownership gaps without claiming an exhaustive audit.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Assess service capability and exposure

**Learn:**

- Service performance
- Dependencies
- Information risk

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-glpi).

**Practise:** Use a synthetic service and asset inventory to practise tracing a critical service to its dependencies. Combine this with operational measures and an explicit list of risks requiring specialist review.

**Advance when:** The case identifies a critical dependency, a service weakness and responsible decision owners; the GLPI exercise is not presented as a complete risk assessment.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Compare investments and governance choices

**Learn:**

- Portfolio prioritisation
- Financial assumptions
- Decision rights

**Resource still needed:** Verified training in technology portfolio finance, information governance and executive decision-making remains a resource gap.

**Practise:** Compare three investments against organisational goals, cost assumptions, risk and expected benefits. Draft decision rights and escalation routes for funding and information policy.

**Advance when:** Your recommendation states the preferred portfolio, rejected alternatives, uncertain assumptions and how benefits will be inspected.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Create and challenge a strategic roadmap

**Learn:**

- Sequenced change
- Accountability
- Outcome review

**Resource still needed:** A verified executive information-systems curriculum and supervised organisational practice are needed beyond this editorial case exercise.

**Practise:** Assemble a multi-horizon roadmap with dependencies, accountable owners, service continuity plans and review measures. Ask experienced operational and business reviewers to challenge the proposal.

**Advance when:** The strategy pack connects current-state evidence, investment decisions, governance and measurable review points; any simulated review is labelled.

## After this recipe

Pursue progressively accountable information-services leadership experience; a strategy case is learning evidence, not a substitute for executive responsibility.
