# Learning curriculum: Change Control Manager

Assess a proposed service change and record a justified approval, rejection or deferral with a rollback plan.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Operations, platforms and IT support](professions-operations.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Change Control Manager](professions-operations.md#change-control-manager)

**Starting point:** Prior practical experience required

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Have experience coordinating operational changes and affected stakeholders.
- Understand service dependencies and incident impact.

**What you will produce:** A fictional change package with risk assessment, approval conditions, verification and closure evidence.

**Resource coverage:** 1 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Frame the change request

**Learn:**

- Purpose and scope
- Affected services and owners

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-glpi).

**Practise:** Create an invented service change and identify its intended benefit, scope, affected users and decision owner.

**Advance when:** A reviewer can distinguish the requested change from the problem motivating it; the tool is not the approval policy.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Assess implementation risk

**Learn:**

- Dependency impact
- Rollback and verification

**Resource still needed:** No reviewed change-control or ITIL procedure is catalogued; the risk rubric is an explicit editorial practice assumption.

**Practise:** Compare a normal rollout with a failure scenario and write implementation, validation and rollback steps.

**Advance when:** Each significant risk has a control, an accountable person and evidence needed before approval.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Coordinate the decision

**Learn:**

- Maintenance windows
- Approval and communication records

**Resource still needed:** A reviewed source for change authorities and organisation-specific approval rules is missing.

**Practise:** Run a tabletop review with fictional stakeholders and record approval, rejection or deferral and any conditions.

**Advance when:** The decision names its authority, service constraints and communication plan rather than relying on a meeting alone.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone: close a simulated change

**Learn:**

- Outcome review
- Exception and rollback records

**Resource still needed:** The capstone is a management simulation, not proof of a production change or conformity to a named service-management framework.

**Practise:** Walk through the change plan, inject one failed validation result and decide whether to proceed, pause or roll back.

**Advance when:** The closure record traces the decision to evidence and confirms the service state after the exercise.

## After this recipe

Use the employer's actual policy and supervised change reviews before authorising modifications to shared services.
