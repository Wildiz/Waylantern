## Selected repositories

- [500 Lines or Less](https://github.com/aosabook/500lines): Architecture of Open Source Applications book explaining design decisions and tradeoffs through walkthroughs of programs implemented in at most 500 source lines each. (intermediate; Free)
- [Markdown Architectural Decision Records (MADR)](https://github.com/adr/madr): Markdown templates and guidance for recording the context, considered options, outcome and consequences of an architectural decision, with examples from the project itself. (intermediate; Free)
