# Learning curriculum: Information Security Compliance Consultant

A security-obligation mapping recipe that separates an actual requirement from a suggested control or an unsupported compliance claim.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Cybersecurity, audit and risk](professions-security.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Information Security Compliance Consultant](professions-security.md#information-security-compliance-consultant)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Basic security-control knowledge.
- Ability to read scoped requirements and distinguish evidence from assertions.

**What you will produce:** A mock security-obligation matrix with evidence requests, gaps, responsible owners and clearly bounded conclusions.

**Resource coverage:** 1 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Understand security-control context](#stage-1)
2. [Translate an agreed requirement](#stage-2)
3. [Review evidence and gaps](#stage-3)
4. [Present a bounded advisory report](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand security-control context

**Learn:**

- Policies, controls and assurance evidence
- Requirement scope and applicability

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Professor Messer (CompTIA Network+ and Security+ videos)](https://www.professormesser.com/security-plus/sy0-701/sy0-701-video/sy0-701-comptia-security-plus-course/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-devops.md#resource-professor-messer).

**Practise:** Describe a fictional service and its information types, then list which parties would need to decide its obligations.

**Advance when:** You distinguish a legal question requiring qualified advice from a technical control question.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Translate an agreed requirement

**Learn:**

- Requirement wording and applicability
- Control objectives and evidence mapping

**Resource still needed:** Current laws, security standards and jurisdiction-specific interpretations have not been verified; fictional clauses are used only to practise mapping.

**Practise:** Use three explicitly fictional contractual security clauses to create an obligation-to-control matrix.

**Advance when:** Each row retains the exact practice clause and explains the proposed evidence without inventing a standard citation.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Review evidence and gaps

**Learn:**

- Sufficient evidence and limitations
- Exceptions, remediation and validation

**Resource still needed:** No verified certification or security-compliance audit methodology is provided; do not issue compliance claims from this exercise.

**Practise:** Review synthetic access-review and backup records against the mock clauses; distinguish absent evidence from proven failure.

**Advance when:** The gap register identifies the requirement, evidence reviewed, limitation, owner and next verification action.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Present a bounded advisory report

**Learn:**

- Stakeholder communication and change monitoring
- Action tracking and review decisions

**Resource still needed:** This editorial consultation simulation is not legal advice, accreditation or an attestation of compliance.

**Practise:** Deliver the mapping and gap register with a plain-language summary, assumptions and a plan for rechecking obligations.

**Advance when:** A reviewer can identify exactly which fictional clauses were assessed and which conclusions remain unsupported.

## After this recipe

Select the real contractual, regulatory or standard context with a qualified specialist and verify its current primary sources before advising clients.
