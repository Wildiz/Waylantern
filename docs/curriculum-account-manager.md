# Learning curriculum: Account Manager

Understand a customer use case, coordinate service commitments and review relationship health.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Leadership, product and technology relationships](professions-leadership.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Account Manager](professions-leadership.md#account-manager)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Can explain a technology service and communicate with customer and delivery stakeholders.
- Can separate a customer request from a contractual commitment and knows when to involve authorised specialists.

**What you will produce:** A fictional account plan with a needs brief, service follow-up log and evidence-based review.

**Resource coverage:** 2 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Understand the customer goal and service context

**Learn:**

- Customer outcomes
- Stakeholder map
- Service boundaries

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Open Practice Library](https://github.com/openpracticelibrary/openpracticelibrary) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-design-product.md#resource-open-practice-library).

**Practise:** Use an invented customer account to map the goal, users, decision-makers and the service currently provided. Adapt the discovery practice to record needs and assumptions without turning an empathy map into a sales forecast.

**Advance when:** The brief distinguishes the customer outcome, the delivered service and questions that need confirmation.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Learn commercial and contractual responsibilities

**Learn:**

- Commitment authority
- Service terms
- Renewal and pricing context

**Resource still needed:** A verified account-management curriculum and applicable contract, procurement and commercial-practice sources are not catalogued.

**Practise:** For a fictional service agreement, identify which requests are included, which need a change decision and who can authorise pricing or terms. Record questions for qualified commercial or legal reviewers.

**Advance when:** You can explain the boundary between coordinating a request and making an unauthorised commercial commitment.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Coordinate a service problem and follow-up

**Learn:**

- Escalation
- Ownership
- Customer communication

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-glpi).

**Practise:** Create a synthetic service issue and a follow-up log in plain text or an optional GLPI lab. Record the customer impact, delivery owner, next update and the evidence required before calling it resolved.

**Advance when:** The log shows who is doing the work, what the customer has been told and how resolution will be confirmed; using a ticket tool is not treated as learning the whole role.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Review the account and prioritise actions

**Learn:**

- Relationship health
- Outcome evidence
- Improvement planning

**Resource still needed:** Verified training in account strategy, commercial negotiation and relationship management remains a learning-resource gap.

**Practise:** Prepare a fictional account review combining the customer goal, service evidence, unresolved concerns and next actions. Separate measured outcomes from assumptions about satisfaction, renewal or revenue.

**Advance when:** The capstone contains a needs brief, responsibility map, service follow-up record and a justified next-action plan with owners.

## After this recipe

Practise with supervised customer work and appropriate commercial training; do not use a simulated review as evidence of real account ownership.
