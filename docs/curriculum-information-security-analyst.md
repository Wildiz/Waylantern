# Learning curriculum: Information Security Analyst

A risk-analysis recipe linking a small information service to feasible controls, evidence and reassessment.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Cybersecurity, audit and risk](professions-security.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Information Security Analyst](professions-security.md#information-security-analyst)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Basic security and system concepts.
- Ability to explain a business process in plain language.

**What you will produce:** An evidence-based risk assessment for a fictional service with treatment choices and a review plan.

**Resource coverage:** 2 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Model the information service](#stage-1)
2. [Assess plausible risks](#stage-2)
3. [Select and test a control](#stage-3)
4. [Communicate the assessment](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Model the information service

**Learn:**

- Information flows, ownership and dependencies
- Threats, weaknesses and consequences

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Professor Messer (CompTIA Network+ and Security+ videos)](https://www.professormesser.com/security-plus/sy0-701/sy0-701-video/sy0-701-comptia-security-plus-course/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-devops.md#resource-professor-messer).
- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-web.md#resource-owasp-top-10).

**Practise:** Diagram a fictional appointment service using only invented information; mark trust boundaries and business dependencies.

**Advance when:** You can explain how one weakness might affect confidentiality, integrity or availability without treating the risk as a confirmed incident.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Assess plausible risks

**Learn:**

- Likelihood assumptions and impact
- Evidence quality and uncertainty

**Resource still needed:** No verified organization-specific risk methodology is provided; the risk scenarios and ratings are editorial exercises.

**Practise:** Write three risk scenarios and list what evidence would strengthen or weaken each estimate.

**Advance when:** Each scenario connects a cause, an event and a business consequence; uncertainty is recorded.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Select and test a control

**Learn:**

- Risk treatment and ownership
- Control effectiveness and remaining exposure

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Linux Upskill Challenge](https://github.com/livialima/linuxupskillchallenge) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-linux-upskill-challenge).

**Practise:** Choose one lab access or backup control related to a scenario and compare its expected protection with a small reproducible test.

**Advance when:** The control recommendation states what the test demonstrates and what it cannot prevent.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Communicate the assessment

**Learn:**

- Priorities and decision records
- Monitoring indicators and reassessment triggers

**Resource still needed:** No profession-specific information-security analyst curriculum or assessment has been verified; this is a bounded editorial capstone.

**Practise:** Create a short assessment with risk scenarios, test evidence, owners, treatment options and a review trigger for each decision.

**Advance when:** A nontechnical reviewer can identify what decision is requested, its assumptions and the remaining risk.

## After this recipe

Have an experienced analyst review your assumptions, then learn the risk and control processes of a supervised organization.
