# Learning curriculum: Business Intelligence Analyst

An editorial route from a decision question to a reconciled business-intelligence analysis.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Data, analytics and artificial intelligence](professions-data-ai.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Business Intelligence Analyst](professions-data-ai.md#bi-analyst)

**Starting point:** Start with the basics

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Basic computer and file handling
- Arithmetic with percentages and ratios
- Interest in a specific business process

**What you will produce:** A small metric brief and dashboard interpretation with traceable numbers and explicit decision limits.

**Resource coverage:** 4 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Define a decision and its metric](#stage-1)
2. [Retrieve and reconcile business data](#stage-2)
3. [Build and interpret a focused view](#stage-3)
4. [Capstone - deliver a decision brief](#stage-4)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Define a decision and its metric

**Learn:**

- Decision context
- Numerator denominator and time window
- Data availability and caveats

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Microsoft Data Science for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/Data-Science-For-Beginners) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-microsoft-data-science-for-beginners).

**Practise:** Write a decision question and define one metric precisely before opening a visualization tool.

**Advance when:** Calculate the metric on a few hand-worked examples and explain how an altered denominator changes its meaning.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Retrieve and reconcile business data

**Learn:**

- SQL filters and grouping
- Joins and duplicate measures
- Data completeness

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [SQLBolt](https://sqlbolt.com/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-sqlbolt).
- [PostgreSQL Exercises](https://pgexercises.com/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-postgresql-exercises).

**Practise:** Answer the question on a practice dataset with saved SQL, including a check for missing or duplicated records.

**Advance when:** Reconcile one result to the input rows and explain how a join or filter affects the denominator.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Build and interpret a focused view

**Learn:**

- Chart selection
- Segmentation and comparison
- Correlation and decision uncertainty

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [From Data to Viz](https://www.data-to-viz.com/) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-from-data-to-viz).
- [Microsoft Learn: Power BI training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi) — beginner in this topic; freemium access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-power-bi-microsoft-learn).

**Practise:** Create a small chart or local dashboard showing the metric over time or across relevant segments.

**Advance when:** Explain a change using actual underlying records and separate an observed pattern from a proposed cause.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone - deliver a decision brief

**Learn:**

- Evidence and recommendation
- Metric definitions and freshness
- Limitations and follow-up questions

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [storytelling with data: free resources](https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/resources) — beginner in this topic; freemium access. [Details](area-data.md#resource-storytelling-with-data-resources).

**Practise:** Deliver a one-page brief with the question, metric, reconciled chart, proposed action and conditions that could change the recommendation.

**Advance when:** A reader can trace the main number to saved queries and identify both the recommended action and the unresolved uncertainty.

## After this recipe

Add business-domain knowledge and repeated stakeholder review; a dashboard alone does not establish a sound business decision.
