# Learning curriculum: Incident Analyst

Turn a reported symptom into a reproducible case with evidence and a clear diagnostic next step.

[Home](../README.md) / [Curricula](curricula.md) / [Operations, platforms and IT support](professions-operations.md#roles-in-this-group)

**Target profile:** [Incident Analyst](professions-operations.md#incident-analyst)

**Starting point:** Learn the stated foundations first

**Type:** Waylantern editorial learning recipe. Related official sources are listed separately below.

## Before you start

- Understand common support requests and simple system checks.
- Use fictional data or a disposable lab.

**What you will produce:** A classified incident record with evidence, a tested hypothesis and a justified escalation or resolution.

**Resource coverage:** 2 of 4 stages have no recorded resource gap. Read the marked gaps before planning your study.

Resource access labels refer to the material or software. Open a resource's details to check prerequisites, hardware, APIs and other running costs before using it.

## Your sequence

1. [Capture a useful report](#stage-1)
2. [Gather diagnostic evidence](#stage-2)

<a id="stage-1"></a>
## 1. Capture a useful report

**Learn:**

- Symptoms and reproduction
- Impact versus assumed cause

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [GLPI](https://github.com/glpi-project/glpi) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-glpi).

**Practise:** Create a fictional ticket and ask what changed, who is affected and how the failure can be repeated.

**Advance when:** The record includes the symptom and reproduction conditions without labelling a guess as the cause.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 2](#stage-2).

<a id="stage-2"></a>
## 2. Gather diagnostic evidence

**Learn:**

- System status and logs
- Time and environment context

**Study resources** — consult the sections needed for this task; the resources may teach complementary skills:

- [Linux Upskill Challenge](https://github.com/livialima/linuxupskillchallenge) — beginner in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-linux-upskill-challenge).
- [Microsoft Windows Troubleshooting Documentation](https://github.com/MicrosoftDocs/SupportArticles-docs/tree/main/support/windows-client) — intermediate in this topic; free access. [Details](area-it-support.md#resource-microsoft-windows-troubleshooting).

**Practise:** Choose one relevant operating-system case and collect a small status/log excerpt from an isolated practice system.

**Advance when:** The evidence records time and environment and excludes private identifiers and credentials.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 3](#stage-3).

<a id="stage-3"></a>
## 3. Test and document a hypothesis

**Learn:**

- Expected versus observed result
- Escalation boundaries

**Resource still needed:** A reviewed general incident-analysis method is missing; product-specific articles must not be treated as universal diagnostic recipes.

**Practise:** Write two possible explanations and perform one reversible authorised lab check that helps distinguish them.

**Advance when:** The result supports or weakens a hypothesis and identifies what information is still missing.

After the checkpoint, [continue to stage 4](#stage-4).

<a id="stage-4"></a>
## 4. Capstone: hand off a complete incident

**Learn:**

- Resolution verification
- Receiver-ready notes

**Resource still needed:** This is an editorial evidence-and-handover assignment rather than a complete incident-analyst qualification.

**Practise:** Finish the ticket with a functional recovery test or an escalation containing the exact next question and supporting evidence.

**Advance when:** A second reader can continue the case without repeating all initial information gathering.

## After this recipe

Practise real tickets under supervision and learn the organisation's priority, escalation and data-handling rules.
